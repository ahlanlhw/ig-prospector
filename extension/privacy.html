Privacy Policy — Sprill

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Operated by Willow Tech

Sprill is a Chrome extension that helps businesses capture public Instagram profiles, organize them, draft and send personalized outreach, and export your list. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with data. How much of the sending the software does on your behalf depends on settings you control — see section 2 of the Terms.

What the extension accesses

  • Public profile information you choose to capture on Instagram pages you are viewing — handle, display name, bio, follower count, account type, category, public links, and any public contact shown.
  • Handles collected during a recording session. If you start a session, then for the duration you chose Sprill collects the handles and display names visible on the Instagram pages you browse, without asking each time. It reads only what is already rendered on your screen, runs only on instagram.com, stops on its own when the timer expires, and can be stopped early at any time. It does not click, follow, message, or interact with anything while it does this.
  • Public engagement numbers shown on a profile's post grid — the view, like and comment counts and post dates Instagram already displays — so Sprill can compare an account's posts against that same account's median and flag the ones that overperformed. It stores at most two post links and their numbers per lead. It does not open posts, and it does not store captions, images, or comments other people wrote.
  • Content you create — your ideal-customer keywords, message templates, drafts, notes, and settings.
  • Outreach you send through the tool — the message text, timestamp, and which of your accounts sent it, so you can track it. This includes comments you post through Sprill: the text, the link to the post, and which of your accounts posted it.

Pages Sprill opens for you

Some features work by opening an Instagram page in a temporary browser tab, reading it, and closing it again. This happens without a separate prompt each time, so you should know where it occurs:

  • Enriching a lead — opens a public profile in a background tab to read the bio and score it. This runs automatically after a page scan (up to the "auto-enrich" number in Settings, 10 by default) and after a recording session.
  • Checking read status — opens a message thread in a background tab to see whether a message you sent has been read.
  • One-click and batch send — if you enable them, opens a tab on the recipient's profile, navigates to the message thread, and clicks Send.

These tabs load public Instagram pages in your own browser under your own session, and are closed immediately afterward. Nothing is sent to us.

Finding a lead's best-performing posts opens nothing at all. It reads the numbers already on the profile grid Sprill was looking at anyway — no extra page loads, no post pages.

Where your data is stored

By default, all of your data stays local in your browser (Chrome's extension storage on your device). Sprill works fully offline, with no account and no server involved.

Three features send data off your device. All are optional and off until you turn them on:

  • Google sign-in & Sheets export. Signs you in with Google and writes your list to a spreadsheet that Sprill creates in your own Google Drive. We request only the drive.file scope, which limits Sprill to files it created itself — it cannot read your other Drive documents.
  • Cloud backup. If you choose “Back up to cloud”, a copy of your settings, captured prospects, and outreach log is written to a Firebase Firestore database operated by Willow Tech, stored under your own signed-in user id. Security rules restrict every record to the user who created it, so no other user can read your data. This is a push-only backup so you can restore your own list; we do not analyse it, share it, or use it for any other purpose.
  • Shared lead lists. If you create or join a shared list, the lead details you push to it (handle, profile URL, public profile information, score, and which of your accounts captured it) are written to the same Firebase Firestore database and become readable by every member of that list. Your messages, outreach history, follow-up schedule, and per-lead status are never shared. Membership works by share code: anyone who has the code can join the list and read what is in it, so only share a code with people you intend to give access to. Members cannot be removed individually — to cut off access, create a new list. Leaving a list on your device does not delete leads you already pushed to it.

What we do NOT collect or do

  • We never ask for or store your Instagram password.
  • We never read, export, or transmit your Instagram session cookies or tokens.
  • We do not collect private-account content or private messages you didn't send through the tool.
  • We do not read or store comments other people wrote, or the content of any post.
  • We do not track your general browsing history.
  • Recording sessions never run outside instagram.com, never read private accounts, direct messages, or anything beyond the public handles and profile fields listed above — and never run unless you started one.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data you send to third parties (only if you enable it)

If you enable the optional Google Sheets export, data is sent only to the destination you specify — your own Google Sheet. You control that connection and can remove it at any time. CSV export writes a file to your own device and sends nothing anywhere.

Your controls

  • Delete everything locally: Settings → “Clear all data” removes all stored data immediately.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local data from your browser.
  • Disconnect Google: Settings → “Sign out” revokes Sprill's stored token. You can also remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your cloud backup: use the Contact link on sprill.app, from the address you signed in with and we will delete your stored copy. Note that uninstalling the extension does not by itself remove a backup you previously pushed.
  • Delete a shared list: the list's creator can delete it. If you are a member but not the creator, use the Contact link on sprill.app, from the address you signed in with and we will remove the leads you contributed.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it — there is no expiry and nothing is removed automatically. Uninstalling the extension removes it.
  • Recording sessions: collected handles are held in a temporary staging area only until the session is evaluated or discarded, then cleared. A session left unevaluated for more than 24 hours is discarded automatically, along with everything it collected.
  • Cloud backup and shared lists: kept until you ask us to delete them, using the controls above. We do not delete them on a schedule, because they exist so you can restore your own list later.
