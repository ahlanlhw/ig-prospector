What Sprill is

Tells you which of your DMs were actually read. Instagram files a message from someone who doesn't follow you into a Requests folder and notifies nobody, so silence tells you nothing. Sprill reads Instagram's own “Seen” marker on the threads you sent and separates read and ignored from never opened — opposite problems with opposite fixes. It also scores public profiles against keywords you set, and drafts each message for you to confirm.

What it's not: not a bulk-DM bot. No identical blasts, and sending never runs unattended — you confirm the content and the recipient of every message, one at a time, capped at 5 DMs and 10 comments a day.

Sprill reads the same “Seen” marker Instagram already shows you. It cannot see which folder a message landed in, and Instagram exposes no delivery signal — so “read” and “not read yet” are the only two states we report.

Sprill is an independent tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
